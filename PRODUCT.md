## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are 18–35 year olds living in Baku who want to improve their physique, fitness level, and confidence. They arrive looking for a gym that fits their lifestyle, values personal attention, and keeps them motivated to stay consistent.

## Product Purpose

LION GYM is a premium fitness club in Baku that combines modern equipment, professional trainers, and tailored training programs. Success means helping members build strength and a healthier lifestyle — and keep returning because the environment makes the effort feel worth it.

## Positioning

LION GYM stands apart from other Baku gyms through its community and group atmosphere. Beyond equipment and coaching, membership means belonging to a motivating, friendly group — the reason people stay consistent and invite others.

## Operating Context

Members visit a physical club at 123 Fitness küçəsi, Bakı. Club hours are Monday–Sunday, 06:00–23:00. They choose between Starter, Standard, and Premium membership plans (30 / 50 / 80 AZN per month) and can reach the club by phone, WhatsApp, email, or Instagram. Trainers offer group sessions, personal coaching, and nutrition guidance.

## Product Principles

- Community is the differentiator — group atmosphere and belonging come first.
- Motivation and consistency matter as much as equipment.
- Premium quality: modern equipment, expert coaches, tailored programs.
- Personal attention for every member, at every fitness level.
- Honest, real facts — never fabricated testimonials or claims.
